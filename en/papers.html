Working Papers

Instrumental Variable Estimation of Risk-Neutral Density using Functional Linear Models
with Marine Carrasco and René Garcia

Nonparametric estimation of the risk-neutral density from a time series of European option prices, in which the strike is endogenous because the exchange sets strikes in response to the same shocks that move prices. We develop an instrumental-variable Hermite sieve estimator and a confidence band for the entire density. Application to S&P 500 options (OptionMetrics, 1996–2022).

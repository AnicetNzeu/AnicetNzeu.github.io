Anicet Nzeukoc

PhD Candidate in Economics, Université de Montréal · Member of CIREQ

My research fields are econometrics and financial econometrics: functional data analysis, structural breaks, option pricing and risk-neutral densities, and machine learning methods for structural models.

I study how to recover, from option prices, the probability distribution that markets assign to future asset prices — the risk-neutral density — and how to detect when these market expectations change. With my supervisors Marine Carrasco and René Garcia, I estimate the risk-neutral density from a time series of option prices in which the strike price is endogenous, using an instrumental-variable sieve estimator. I am also working on structural changes in estimated risk-neutral densities and, with Rostand Tchouakam, on deep surrogates for option-pricing models.

Before my PhD, I trained as a Statistical Engineer (ISE) at ISSEA and earned master's degrees in economics (University of Yaoundé II) and applied mathematics (University of Dschang). My doctoral research is supported by the Fonds de recherche du Québec – Société et culture (FRQSC).

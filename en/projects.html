The code for my ongoing research projects is kept in private repositories while the papers are in progress. Applied projects are public and linked below. All public repositories are listed automatically at the bottom of this page.

Research projects

Structural Changes in Risk-Neutral Densities

Detecting and dating structural changes in sequences of estimated risk-neutral densities.

Private repository

Risk-Neutral Density by Instrumental Variables

Instrumental-variable Hermite sieve estimation of risk-neutral densities from S&P 500 options with endogenous strikes.

Python · Private repository

Deep Surrogates for Option Pricing

Neural-network surrogates of structural option-pricing models for risk-neutral density estimation, with Rostand Tchouakam.

Python · Private repository

Proxy-Circular Data Extraction

Automated pipeline that builds a firm-year panel on governance, D&O insurance and executive compensation for 220+ Canadian public firms (2013–2024).

Python · Private repository

Applied projects

Credit Risk Modelling on a Consumer Loan Portfolio

Retail credit risk pipeline built end to end on 887 440 matured consumer loans issued between 2007 and 2015: probability of default, loss given default, exposure at default, the expected credit loss that follows from the three, and the credit scorecard. Weight-of-evidence binning, out-of-sample and out-of-time validation, and intercept recalibration.

Python · Public repository
